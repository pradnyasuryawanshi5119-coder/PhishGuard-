Common Types of Scams

Learn about different types of online threats.

📧

Email Phishing

Fake emails that try to trick you into clicking links or sharing personal information.

📱

SMS Scam

Suspicious text messages asking you to click links or provide sensitive information.

💳

Payment Fraud

Fake payment requests or attempts to obtain your banking information.

💼

Job Scam

Fake job offers that may ask for money or personal information.

📦

Delivery Scam

Fake delivery messages asking you to pay a small amount or click a link.

🎁

Prize Scam

Messages claiming that you won a prize and asking for payment or information.

🔗

Fake Websites

Websites designed to look legitimate but created to steal information.

📲

Social Media Scam

Fake accounts, messages or offers designed to trick users.

🚨 Warning Signs

⚠️ Urgent messages
🔗 Unknown links
🔐 Requests for OTP/PIN
💰 Unexpected payment requests
